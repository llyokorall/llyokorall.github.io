What Buszi does

📍
Nearby, at a glanceOpen the app and see every line around you with its next departures.
🟢
Live timesReal-time arrivals where the agency publishes them, refreshed about every 30 seconds.
🗺️
Buses on the mapWatch buses move along their street, and see how full they are when the agency shares it.
🧭
Plan and goTrip planner with walking and transfers. Tap Start and Buszi buzzes when it's time to get off.
⏰
RemindersA nudge 2, 5 or 10 minutes before your bus leaves, following delays.
📶
Works offlineThe timetable is stored on your phone. Data saver for mobile data.

En français

Buszi vous indique quand passe votre prochain bus ou train, gratuitement et sans compte : arrêts à proximité, heures en direct là où elles sont publiées, bus sur la carte, planificateur de trajet et rappels. L'appli est offerte en français et en anglais.

Free and private

Buszi is free. It shows at most one short ad when you open the app, no more than once every few hours. No account, no sign-up. Your location stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Buszi is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by any city or transit agency. Transit data comes from each agency's open data, used under its licence (full list in the app). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, map tiles by OpenFreeMap.
