Privacy Policy: Buszi

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Contact: alicehouseapp@gmail.com

Buszi ("the app") is an independent, free public-transit app. It is not affiliated with the City of Edmonton or any transit agency.

Information the app uses

Location. If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to show nearby stops and departures. Location is processed on your device only. We do not collect, store or sell it, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Pinned lines. Lines you pin are stored on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, they may be included in your own Google backup.

Requests to transit and map servers. To show schedules, real-time arrivals and maps, the app downloads data from:

  • the open-data servers of the transit agency of the region you choose, for transit data (for example City of Edmonton: gtfs.edmonton.ca; BC Transit: bct.tmix.se; TransLink: gtfs-static.translink.ca);
  • GitHub (github.com and its download servers), for prebuilt timetable files where available;
  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org, served via Cloudflare), for map tiles.

Reminders. Departure reminders you set are scheduled and shown on your device only.

Like any website, these servers receive your IP address and the map area being viewed. We do not control them.

Advertising. The app shows one ad when you open it, using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and usage and diagnostic information to serve, personalize (where permitted) and measure ads. Where required by law, we ask for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in the app under About & privacy → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not have accounts, and we do not collect your name, email or contacts.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use background location.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights (Canada)

Under PIPEDA and applicable provincial laws (including Québec's Law 25), you may ask what information we hold about you and request correction or deletion. Because the app itself does not collect personal information on our servers, most requests are handled through Google (for ads) or by uninstalling the app. Contact: alicehouseapp@gmail.com.

Changes
